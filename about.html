Our Story

Secular and Theology Education, Side by Side

Raayat Islamic Nursery & Primary School is a day school on Salaama–Munyonyo Road in Makindye, Kampala. We opened our doors to serve families in this neighbourhood who wanted one school to do two things properly: teach the national primary syllabus well, and raise children who know their faith.

Our name sits above a crest with an open book beneath the crescent, and a motto we take seriously — Allah is the key to knowledge. It is a reminder to staff and pupils alike that learning is an act of worship, and that character counts as much as marks.

Today the school runs from Baby Class through to Primary Seven, following Uganda’s Competency Based Curriculum, with Qur’an, Arabic and Islamic Studies woven through every week of the timetable rather than added on at the edges.

Mission, Vision & Motto

01

Our Mission

To give every child a sound academic foundation and a living understanding of Islam, so they leave Primary Seven confident in class, in the masjid and at home.

02

Our Vision

A school where no child is left behind academically or spiritually — known by name, followed up closely, and prepared for secondary school and beyond.

03

Our Motto

Allah is the key to knowledge. Everything we teach begins from that conviction, and every lesson is meant to deepen it rather than compete with it.

Our Values

What We Teach Beyond the Syllabus

Five habits we work on with every pupil, every term.

Taqwa

God-consciousness in how a child speaks, studies and treats others — whether or not a teacher is watching.

Discipline

Punctuality, clean uniform, completed homework and respect for elders, corrected gently and consistently.

Curiosity

Asking questions, trying again after a mistake, and taking pleasure in finding something out for oneself.

Service

Looking after the compound, helping a struggling classmate, and taking responsibility without being asked.

Honesty

Telling the truth about homework, about conduct and about mistakes — the habit that makes all the others possible.

Places for Nursery to Primary Seven

We accept new pupils into Baby, Middle and Top Class, and into Primary One through Primary Seven where places are available, as day pupils or boarders. Children joining mid-stream are placed after a short assessment so they land in the class that fits them.

Registration runs ahead of each term. The earlier you come in, the more likely your preferred class still has room.

Step by Step

How to Register

Five steps from first call to first day.

Get in touch

Call 0704 178474 or email raayatislamicschool02@gmail.com and tell us your child’s age and the class you have in mind.

Visit the school

Come to the school on Salaama–Munyonyo Road during school hours. You will see the classrooms and compound and meet the office.

Collect and fill the form

Pick up an admission form from the office, complete it and return it with the documents listed below.

Placement check

Pupils joining Primary One and above sit a short placement assessment so we put them in the right class. Nursery entrants simply meet the class teacher.

Confirm the place

Settle the registration requirements with the office, collect the requirements list and uniform details, and your child is expected on the first day of term.

Bring With You

What You Will Need

Bring copies and keep the originals. If something is missing, come anyway and talk to the office — we would rather help you sort it out than turn a child away.

  • The child’s birth certificate or baptism/naming record
  • Two or three passport-size photographs
  • Immunisation card (for nursery entrants)
  • The most recent school report, for pupils transferring in
  • A transfer or release letter from the previous school, where one applies
  • Parent or guardian’s contact details and national ID

Requirements can change between terms. Please confirm the current list with the school office before you come.

School Fees

Fees Structure

Fees differ by class and are reviewed each year, so we issue the current structure directly rather than publishing figures that may go out of date.

What school fees cover

  • Tuition for both the CBC and theology strands
  • Continuous assessment, examinations and termly reports
  • Use of classroom materials and school facilities
  • Supervision throughout the school day

Usually charged separately

  • Uniform, including sports and prayer wear
  • Scholastic materials on the requirements list
  • Lunch, where taken at school
  • Trips, special events and PLE registration for candidates

Tell us which class your child is joining and we will send you the fees for this term, together with the payment details and the requirements list.

Questions

Frequently Asked

What age should my child be for Baby Class?

Nursery entry depends on the child’s readiness as much as age. Talk to the office about your child specifically — the class teacher will meet them before placement.

Do you take non-Muslim pupils?

Raayat is an Islamic school and the theology strand is part of the timetable for all pupils. Families who are comfortable with that are welcome to discuss admission with the office.

Is the school day or boarding?

Both. Day pupils report in the morning and go home at the end of the school day, and we also take boarders. Talk to the office about boarding places, what a boarder needs to bring and the fees that apply.

Can my child join in the middle of a term?

Yes, where a place is available. A short placement assessment makes sure your child joins the class that suits them.

How do I get the fees structure?

Call the office on 0704 178474 or send an enquiry from the contact page, naming the class your child will join.

Where exactly is the school?

On Salaama–Munyonyo Road in Makindye, Kampala. Call the office and they will give you the nearest landmark and directions from your side of town.

Our Story

Welcome to Raayat Islamic School

Raayat Islamic Nursery & Primary School is a day school on Salaama–Munyonyo Road in Makindye, Kampala. We teach the national primary syllabus and Islamic theology together, so that a child leaves us literate, numerate, curious — and grounded in faith and good character.

Our classes run from Baby, Middle and Top Class through Primary One to Primary Seven. Lessons follow Uganda’s Competency Based Curriculum, while Qur’an, Arabic and Islamic Studies run through every week of the school calendar.

  • Qur’an & Arabic
  • CBC lessons
  • Daily prayers
  • Small classes
  • Day school

Our Programme

Journey of Education at Raayat

Three stages, one path — from a child’s first day in Baby Class to the PLE desk in Primary Seven.

Baby · Middle · Top

Nursery Section

The first years are about settling in, speaking up and learning to love school. Children play, sing, count and start forming letters — and learn their first short surahs and du’as alongside them.

  • Baby, Middle and Top Class
  • Letters, numbers, colours and shapes through play
  • Short surahs, du’as and good manners
  • Preparation for a confident start in Primary One
Read More →
P.1 – P.3

Lower Primary

Reading, writing and number work become solid skills. Lessons follow the Competency Based Curriculum, with plenty of practice and group activity, while Qur’an and Arabic keep their place in the weekly timetable.

  • Literacy, Numeracy, English and Religious Education
  • Activity based CBC lessons
  • Qur’an recitation and Arabic
  • Regular continuous assessment and reports to parents
Read More →
P.4 – P.7

Upper Primary

The examinable subjects take centre stage and pupils are prepared steadily for the Primary Leaving Examination — with revision, past papers and close follow up of each candidate.

  • English, Mathematics, Science and Social Studies
  • Islamic Studies, Qur’an and Arabic
  • Structured PLE preparation in P.6 and P.7
  • Guidance on secondary school choices
